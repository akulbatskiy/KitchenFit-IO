# KitchenFit IO — original idea and Variant 2

## Original idea

A focused tool for commercial kitchen fit-out contractors that starts from the final client handover pack and works backward into structured capture.

## Original value proposition

Help a small contractor turn messy project delivery into a professional, traceable, client-ready handover package without heavy project software.

## Variant 2

A narrower AI-assisted workflow for kitchen design and engineering reports.

This variant emerged from a live customer signal where the potential client said AI reduced a report workflow from around 2 days to around 2 hours.

## Current hypothesis

The first wedge may be closer to design / engineering report production than to project handover.

## Confirmed facts

- A potential client asked about AI for kitchens.
- He wants to aid kitchen design.
- He described the scope as both design and engineering.
- He uses Manus for kitchen-related reports.
- He reported a major time saving: about 2 days down to about 2 hours.

## Assumptions

- Manus may not be sufficient for repeatable professional workflow.
- A kitchen-specific wrapper may be valuable.
- He may pay for a narrow pilot.
- A rough clickable mock may help clarify the opportunity.

## Open questions

3. What remains manual after Manus?
4. What errors or quality risks remain?
5. How often does he produce these reports?
6. Would he pay for a narrower, repeatable workflow?
