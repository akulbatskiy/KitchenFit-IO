# Decision 0001 — Do not build before customer reply

## Date
2026-05-20

## Status
Accepted

## Context

There is a promising live customer signal around AI-assisted kitchen design / engineering reports. The customer reported a large time saving from Manus, but has not yet shown willingness to pay for a dedicated product.

## Decision

Do not start production development yet.

Prepare discovery questions and wait for workflow details. If the workflow is concrete, prepare a rough clickable mock for a coffee conversation.

## Why

The current signal proves interest and value, but not payment readiness.

## Consequences

Allowed:
- discovery notes;
- mock outline;
- feature spec;
- customer script;
- manual pilot planning.

Blocked:
- broad SaaS build;
- full project-management platform;
- production architecture;
- payments, marketplace, accounting integrations.

## Review trigger

Review after Radford replies or after the coffee conversation.
