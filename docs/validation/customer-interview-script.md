# Customer interview script

## Goal

Understand whether there is a paid workflow behind AI-assisted kitchen design / engineering reports.

## Opening

I am not trying to sell you a finished product. I want to understand the workflow properly and see whether there is a narrow useful tool or service worth testing.

## Questions

1. What exact report did you create in Manus?
2. What did that report take before Manus?
3. What does it take now?
4. What inputs do you use?
5. Which parts still require manual judgement?
6. What would be risky if the AI output was wrong?
7. Who receives the final document?
8. What happens if the document is late, unclear, or incomplete?
9. How often do you produce this type of report?
10. Would you test a kitchen-specific version on one real project?
11. Would you pay for a pilot if it saved time or made the output more repeatable?

## Strong signals

- He shares a real old report.
- He gives real inputs.
- He names a live project.
- He agrees to a paid pilot, deposit, or written commitment.
- He introduces another user or buyer.

## Weak signals

- He says it is interesting but gives no artefacts.
- He cannot name frequency.
- He only wants to brainstorm.
- He wants a free build before committing anything.
