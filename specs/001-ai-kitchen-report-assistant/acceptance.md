# Acceptance — AI kitchen report assistant

## Discovery acceptance

- Customer can explain the workflow without prompting.
- Inputs are known.
- Output recipient is known.
- Remaining pain is known.
- Frequency is known.

## Mock acceptance

- Customer agrees the mock reflects a real workflow.
- Customer identifies missing or wrong parts.
- Customer says whether it would be useful on a live project.

## Pilot acceptance

- Customer provides one real project.
- Success criteria are agreed.
- There is money, deposit, LOI, or meaningful operational commitment.

## Kill signals

- Customer only wants a general AI chat.
- No concrete artefacts are shared.
- Manus is already good enough.
- Customer will not commit time, project, or money.
