# Feature spec — AI kitchen report assistant

## Status
Draft / discovery

## Problem

Commercial kitchen design and engineering reports may take significant expert time to prepare. A potential customer is already using Manus to reduce a report workflow from around 2 days to around 2 hours, but the remaining workflow gaps are unknown.

## User

Commercial kitchen designer / engineer / fit-out professional producing reports for clients, environmental health, consultants, or internal stakeholders.

## Current workflow

Known:
- Uses Manus for reports.
- Needs domain understanding to get useful output.
- Describes the use case as both design and engineering.

Unknown:
- exact inputs;
- report type;
- final recipient;
- remaining manual work;
- quality risks;
- frequency;
- payment readiness.

## Inputs to validate

- Floor plans
- Equipment lists
- Site notes
- Photos
- Measurements
- Engineering assessment notes
- Compliance constraints

## Candidate output

A structured kitchen design / engineering report draft that can be reviewed, edited, and exported.

## Success criteria

Discovery success:
- customer shares a real workflow;
- customer names actual inputs and output recipient;
- customer identifies at least one gap in Manus;
- customer agrees to review a rough clickable mock.

Pilot success:
- customer gives one live project;
- customer agrees success criteria;
- customer pays, deposits, or provides a strong written commitment.

## Non-goals

- Full project-management system
- Marketplace
- Accounting integrations
- Compliance certification replacement
- Broad construction SaaS

## Facts

- One potential customer initiated the AI/kitchen conversation.
- He already uses Manus for reports.
- He reported a 2 days to 2 hours improvement.

## Assumptions

- A repeatable workflow may be valuable beyond general Manus usage.
- Kitchen-specific prompts, templates, and structured inputs may reduce rework.
- A clickable mock may help validate willingness to pay.

## Open questions

1. What does he feed into Manus?
2. Who receives the final document?
3. What still feels clunky?
4. What would he pay for?
5. How often is the workflow repeated?

## Build gate

Current gate: Discovery.

Do not move to build until at least one paid or deposit-backed pilot is secured.
